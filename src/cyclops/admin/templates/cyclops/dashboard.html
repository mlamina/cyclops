{% comment %} One project, and the five things it is. A project folder holds a README, a Log, a workbook, its pictures and whatever else you put there - and until this screen existed they were five rows of a directory listing, told apart only by their filenames. The rail names them instead. One shell for all five, not five screens. The rail and the crumb bar are the fixed part and #pbody is the part each section writes, which is what lets the upload controls below keep the listeners app.js binds to them once at load: a section change rewrites #pbody and never the bar they live in. #pbody wears .files, .doc or .grid depending on what is in it, so the rules those three classes already had are the rules that still apply. {% endcomment %}
